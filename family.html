What to wear

Matching plain colours make the whole family look great together.

  • Pick one colour for everyone, like black, white or blue.
  • Avoid patterns, logos and printed designs.
  • Tell us your outfit colours in advance so we can plan the backdrop.
  • Want to wear shoes? Bring a separate clean pair for the studio.
  • Arrive on time so we can finish without rushing.
  • Photo selection for individual photos is done at our studio.
  • We deliver edited high-resolution photos only. Raw photos aren't shared.

Questions families ask

What should our family wear for the photoshoot?

Matching plain colours look best, such as all black, all white or all blue. Avoid patterns, logos and printed designs.

Can we do the family shoot outdoors?

Yes. Silver and Gold include one location, in our studio or outdoors. Platinum includes up to two nearby locations.

Do you give raw photos for family shoots?

No. We deliver professionally edited, high-resolution photos only. Gold and Platinum also include all good soft copies.

Can I buy individual photos instead of a package?

Yes. Edited photos are ₹300 each, and you choose them at our studio.
