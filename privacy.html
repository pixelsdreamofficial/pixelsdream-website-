Privacy policy

Last updated October 2026

What we collect

When you contact us on WhatsApp or by phone, we receive your name, number and the details you share about your shoot, such as dates, your baby's age or your due date. This website does not have accounts or online payments.

How we use it

We use your details only to plan your shoot, confirm bookings, deliver your photos and reply to your questions. We don't sell or share your details with anyone for marketing.

Your photos

We sometimes share our favourite work on this website and on Instagram. If you'd like your photos kept private, tell us before your shoot and we won't post them. If a photo of your family is already online and you'd like it removed, message us and we'll take it down.

Website analytics

This website may use Google Analytics and Google Ads to understand which pages are useful and which ads bring visitors. These tools use cookies and collect anonymous usage information.
