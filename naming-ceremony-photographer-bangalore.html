Questions parents ask

Do you provide the decor?

No. We are photographers, so our packages cover photography and video only. Decor, cake and venue are arranged by your family or your event planner, and we capture all of it beautifully.

How much does naming ceremony photography cost?

Our packages start at ₹7,000 for up to 4 hours with one photographer, up to 25 edited photos and all raw images. Gold is ₹10,000 and Platinum is ₹15,000.

Do we get all the photos?

Yes. Every package includes unlimited soft copies of all raw images, plus edited high-resolution photos and an AI gallery for sharing.

Can you cover the function for longer?

Yes. Extra coverage is ₹1,000 per hour.

Do you cover functions outside Electronic City?

Yes. We cover naming ceremonies and annaprashan at homes, halls and temples across Bangalore.
