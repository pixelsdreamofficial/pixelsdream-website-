Before your baby's shoot

Getting ready

A few simple steps help your baby stay settled and sleepy, which makes for the most beautiful photographs.

  1. Baby's age
    The best time is when your baby is 5–28 days old.
  2. Warm bath
    Give your baby a warm water bath before the shoot.
  3. Well fed
    Make sure your baby is well fed just before the session.
  4. No oil or kajal
    Please avoid oil and kajal on your baby's face and hair.
  5. Remove threads & jewellery
    Take off all threads and jewellery from the neck, hands and feet.
  6. Easy clothes
    Don't swaddle your baby before coming. Use front-button clothes that come off easily, and skip mittens and socks.
  7. What to bring
    A hanky, comb, diapers, extra milk and a pacifier.
  8. Parents' outfits
    Tell us your outfit colours in advance so we can match the setups.
  9. Clean shoes
    If parents want to wear shoes in the photos, bring a separate clean pair.
  10. Reference pictures
    Want a specific look from Pinterest or Instagram? Share it before booking, not during the session.

Questions parents ask

What is the best age for a newborn photoshoot?

Between 5 and 28 days old. Babies sleep deeply and curl up naturally at this age, which makes posing gentle and easy.

Do you offer newborn photoshoots at home?

Yes, for ₹2,000 extra. A few setups are only possible in the studio, so we'll tell you which ones when you book.

Can parents be in the photos?

Yes. Every package includes a family shoot with the parents.

Do I get all the photos?

Yes. Every package includes all raw images, plus the number of edited photos listed in your package.

Can I ask for poses I saw on Instagram or Pinterest?

Yes, please share them with us before booking so we can plan the setups. We can't add new requests once the shoot is booked or during the session.
