    Questions

    What is the difference between 3mm and 5mm?

    Both are crystal-clear acrylic. 5mm is thicker and heavier, with a deeper, more premium glass look, especially on larger sizes. 3mm is lighter and great for smaller frames.

    Are the wall studs included?

    Yes. Every frame price includes a set of steel studs to fix it on the wall. An extra set costs ₹200.

    How do I send my photo?

    After you place the order, attach your photo in the same WhatsApp chat. Send it as a Document, not as a normal photo, so WhatsApp doesn't compress it. If we shot your photos, just tell us the photo number.

    Can I collect the frame from your studio?

    Yes. Pickup from our studio in Electronic City Phase 2 is free. We can also courier it to you; charges depend on your location and we confirm them on WhatsApp.

    Will my photo fit the frame?

    All our sizes have the same shape (2:3), which matches most camera and phone photos. If your photo needs cropping, we'll check it and confirm with you before printing.

    Build your frame above, or message us and we'll help you choose the right size.
