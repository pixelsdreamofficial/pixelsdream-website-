Questions families ask

How much does housewarming photography cost?

Packages start at ₹6,999 for traditional photography up to 4 hours with online gallery delivery. Gold is ₹14,999, Platinum ₹24,999 and Diamond ₹34,999. Extra hours are ₹1,000 each.

Do you cover the early-morning gruhapravesam muhurtham?

Yes. Tell us your muhurtham time when you book and we'll arrive before it starts.

Yes, with the Diamond package. Drone coverage depends on venue permissions and local regulations.

Can you cover the vastu puja or dishti puja the evening before?

Yes. We cover the day-before rituals as a separate 1 to 3 hour session for ₹3,000 extra, added to any package.

Do you cover areas outside Bangalore?

We serve Bangalore and nearby areas. Message us your location and we'll confirm.
