    Before your shoot

    Getting ready

    A few simple steps help your baby stay happy and comfortable during the session.

    1. No oil or kajal
      Please avoid oil and kajal on your baby's face and hair.
    2. Remove threads & jewellery
      Take off all threads and jewellery from the neck, hands and feet.
    3. What to bring
      A hanky, comb, diapers, extra milk and a pacifier.
    4. Parents' outfits
      Tell us your outfit colours in advance. For family photos, matching neutral or dark colours like black, white or blue work best. Avoid patterns and logos.
    5. Clean shoes
      If parents want to wear shoes in the photos, bring a separate clean pair.
    6. Arrive on time
      Late arrivals mean a shorter session, so please come on time and we'll finish without any rush.
