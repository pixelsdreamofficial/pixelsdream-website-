    Questions

    Do the sizes include the frame?

    Yes. Every size is measured including the frame border, and rounded to the nearest inch. For example, an A3 frame is about 13 × 18 inches from edge to edge.

    Which frame colours are available?

    Every size comes in black, brown or white, at the same price. Choose the colour when you build your frame above.

    Can I get it in landscape?

    Yes. Depending on your photo, we can make the artwork in portrait (tall) or landscape (wide). Choose the orientation above and we'll check your photo before printing.

    How do I send my photo?

    After you place the order, attach your photo in the same WhatsApp chat. Send it as a Document, not as a normal photo, so WhatsApp doesn't compress it. If we shot your photos, just tell us the photo number.

    How long does it take?

    Your frame is ready in 24–48 hours after we receive your photo and confirm the order. Delivery time by Porter or courier is extra, depending on your location.

    Can I collect the frame from your studio? What about delivery?

    Pickup from our studio in Electronic City Phase 2 is free. Within Bangalore we can send it by Porter, and outside Bangalore by courier. Porter and courier charges are extra, based on your location, and we confirm them on WhatsApp before dispatch.

    Which size should I choose?

    A5 and A4 suit a desk or shelf. A3 is our most popular size for bedroom and hallway walls. A2 and A1 suit a living room wall, and A0 is a large statement piece above a sofa. Not sure? Message us and we'll help.

    A photo worth hanging?

    Build your frame above, or message us and we'll help you choose the right size.
