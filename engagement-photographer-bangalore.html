Questions couples ask

How much does engagement photography cost?

Semi candid photography starts at ₹7,000 for up to 4 hours with 30 edited photos, all raw photos and an online AI gallery. Photo and video together is ₹15,000.

Do you take couple portraits on the day?

Yes. We set aside a short time for couple portraits, indoors or in the venue garden, without holding up the function.

Do you provide the decor?

No. We are photographers, so we cover photography and video only. Decor and venue are arranged by your family or your event planner.

Do you cover functions outside Electronic City?

Yes. We cover engagements at homes, halls and venues across Bangalore.
